# Illustrated terrain artwork

Generated with the built-in image generation tool for the Illustrated board
prototype. One atlas contains six equal square cells: forest, pasture, and
fields in the first row; hills, mountains, and desert in the second row.
The original generated image is preserved without bitmap edits. SVG viewports
select the cells at display time.

## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: ONE terrain texture atlas for an illustrated online hex board-game prototype. Create a single coherent landscape sheet, 3:2 aspect ratio, ideally 1536 by 1024 pixels, with EXACTLY three equal-width columns and two equal-height rows. The six square panels fill the canvas edge-to-edge with no gutters, borders, frames, text, labels, numbers, icons, hex shapes or watermark. Strict panel order: top-left FOREST, top-middle PASTURE, top-right WHEAT FIELDS; bottom-left CLAY HILLS, bottom-middle MOUNTAINS, bottom-right DESERT.
Visual direction: original warm hand-painted European tabletop strategy-game illustration, polished soft gouache and finely painted natural textures, inviting, tactile, slightly idealized, restrained detail, warm afternoon light, cohesive art direction and lighting. Bird's-eye near-overhead camera, roughly 75 degrees looking down, orthographic terrain vignette; absolutely no sky or horizon. Each panel is a complete square terrain texture to be clipped into a hexagon in the app, so paint right to every edge. Broad readable masses first, fine details second. Keep the central fifth of each panel calmer for a number disc the app will overlay, but do not paint any disc or blank geometric space.
FOREST: deep emerald conifer canopies with some round deciduous trees, golden sunlight on tree crowns, visible earthy paths and mossy ground.
PASTURE: fresh sage and spring-green meadow, a small flock of recognizable ivory sheep with tiny dark heads, clover and softly undulating grass, an unobtrusive rustic fence near a corner.
WHEAT FIELDS: ripe ochre and honey-gold wheat in gently curving planted strips, patches of harvested golden earth, a few wheat sheaves near a corner, clearly farmland.
CLAY HILLS: warm terracotta and burnt-orange exposed clay terraces, earthen tracks, rounded low red escarpments and sparse olive-green scrub, recognizably clay rather than desert.
MOUNTAINS: cool slate-blue and grey rocky peaks, craggy ridges, subtle silver mineral veins and restrained grass in valleys; make the silhouette distinct from hills, no snow-dominated composition.
DESERT: warm pale sand dunes, gentle ripples, a few weathered rocks and extremely sparse scrub, soft cream and sandy gold, visually distinct from wheat.
Constraints: no buildings, castles, people, settlements, roads, game pieces, UI, typography or branded artwork. No photorealistic textures, 3D toy look, cartoon black outlines, neon saturation or dramatic scenery. The six scenes must be equally scaled, consistently detailed and belong to one artist. Exact 3-by-2 equal cell atlas layout is critical.
